The project

Made for one community—not everyone.

George supports the A Mythical Tale community, based around AltaVR titles A Township Tale and Reave. Its features mirror the server’s own activities, giving members useful game-community tools alongside staff moderation and support.

Core features

Community systems under one roof.

  • Auto-moderation
  • Ticketing
  • Levelling
  • Missions
  • Bounties
  • Marketplace listings
  • Player profiles
  • Auto-responders

Command surface

Selected slash commands.

  • /claim · /set-bounty · /close-bountyRun the community bounty workflow.
  • /create-listingList an in-game item for sale.
  • /daily · /weeklyReceive time-based missions.
  • /profileView your own or another member’s profile.
  • /open-ticketStart a private conversation with the moderation team.
