A purpose-built Discord experience for the A Mythical Tale community, connecting moderation with game-inspired missions and community tools.

Overview

One bot for the whole community.

George supports the day-to-day operation of A Mythical Tale while giving members useful, game-focused ways to participate.

  • Auto-moderation
  • Ticketing
  • Level progression
  • Missions
  • Auto-responders
  • Marketplace tools

Commands

Tools tailored to AMT.

  • /claim — Claim a bounty by its identifier
  • /set-bounty — Set a bounty on a user
  • /close-bounty — Close a completed bounty
  • /create-listing — List an in-game item for sale
  • /daily and /weekly — Receive new missions
  • /profile — View your own or another member’s profile
  • /open-ticket — Contact the moderation team
