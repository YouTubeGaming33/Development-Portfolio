George

A guide to the moderation, support, missions, bounties, and marketplace systems built for A Mythical Tale.

01 / Overview

Made for A Mythical Tale.

George supports the A Mythical Tale community around the AltaVR titles A Township Tale and Reave. It brings game-community activities and staff workflows into a single Discord bot.

George is tailored to A Mythical Tale. Its features are designed for members of that community rather than as a publicly installable bot.

02 / Features

Community systems under one roof.

  • Auto-moderation
  • Private ticketing
  • Member levelling
  • Daily and weekly missions
  • Player bounties
  • Marketplace listings
  • Player profiles
  • Auto-responders

03 / Bounties

Run the bounty workflow.

George provides commands for creating, claiming, and closing community bounties.

/set-bounty

Create a new bounty for members of the community.

/claim

Claim an available bounty.

/close-bounty

Close a bounty when its workflow is complete.

04 / Missions

Fresh reasons to take part.

Time-based mission commands give members recurring activities within the community.

/daily

Receive a daily mission.

/weekly

Receive a weekly mission.

05 / Community tools

Profiles, listings, and support.

/profile

View your own profile or another community member’s profile.

/create-listing

List an in-game item for sale in the community marketplace.

/open-ticket

Start a private conversation with the moderation team.
